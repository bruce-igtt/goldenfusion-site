## Publishing rhythm

Publish three organic posts each week, tentatively Monday, Wednesday, and Friday at 10:00 a.m. Eastern. Put the link in the post body and review the wording every four weeks. Use the GFE Page identity, not the personal profile or the IGTT Page. Schedule through Meta Business Suite while a separate GFE Metricool brand is unavailable.

**Launch gate:** Wait until the GFE guide page has a working $4.99 checkout and automatic PDF delivery. The current draft has a launch-notice email button; it is not a live sale. Test a purchase, receipt, download, refund contact, and phone layout before scheduling the posts below.
